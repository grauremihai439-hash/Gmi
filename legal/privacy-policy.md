**Last updated: August 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Chatbot・Ask AI GPT (the "App") processes personal information. The App is an independent AI assistant published by MIHAI ION and powered by the OpenAI API. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or published by OpenAI.

## Information we process

We may process:

- a technical account or user identifier and subscription status;
- prompts, chat messages, uploaded images and documents, and AI-generated responses;
- conversation history that you choose to retain in the App;
- usage information needed to apply daily or monthly limits, such as message counts and token usage;
- content reports, support communications, and limited technical diagnostics needed to operate and secure the service; and
- purchase and entitlement information supplied by Microsoft Store. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request and you have the right to provide it.

## How we use information

We use information to provide AI responses and requested image or document features, maintain conversation history, verify subscription entitlements, enforce usage limits, prevent misuse, investigate content reports, provide support, and maintain the reliability and security of the App.

## AI processing and service providers

Prompts, relevant conversation context, and any files you attach are sent to OpenAI for the purpose of generating a response. OpenAI acts as a service provider for this processing under its applicable business and API terms. Requests are configured not to be stored by the OpenAI Responses API where that configuration is supported.

Microsoft processes Store purchases, subscription management, and related commerce information under Microsoft's own privacy terms. We may also use infrastructure providers to host the App's backend and securely store operational data. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to us.

## Legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information to perform the service you request, to comply with legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in securing, supporting, and improving the reliability of the App. Where consent is required, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

## Retention and deletion

Conversation history is retained until you delete the relevant conversation or ask us to delete your account data. Uploaded file content is transmitted for the requested AI operation and is not intentionally retained by our application after the request, except for the file name or a message indicating that an attachment was used. Generated images may remain in conversation history until that conversation is deleted.

Usage records and limited security or diagnostic records may be retained for up to 90 days, unless a longer period is required to prevent fraud, resolve a dispute, or comply with law. Support communications and content reports may be retained for up to 24 months. Transaction records may be retained as required by tax, accounting, consumer-protection, and other legal obligations.

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion by emailing grauremihai439@gmail.com. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.
